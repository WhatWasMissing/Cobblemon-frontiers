# Myths and Legends Wiki

Welcome to the player and server guide for **Myths and Legends**, the Cobblemon sidemod by D0ctorLeon. The mod adds special items and spawn conditions for Legendary and Mythical Pokémon. This wiki explains the mod, how to find and use key items, the included datapack encounters, commands, configuration, customization, and troubleshooting.

> **Version scope:** The encounter catalogue in this wiki is generated from official datapack commit `cbeea17321cca730b0ff896be1f229c14ef06396`, inspected on 25 September 2026. It describes the official datapack definitions at that commit; a server may use a different datapack version or custom overrides. Item names/descriptions and configuration notes are based on the local Myths and Legends NeoForge 1.9.0 installation. The compatibility notes for **Myths and Legends Direct Spawn Fix** describe the separate server-side patch present in this workspace and do not describe official mod behavior.

## What this wiki covers

Myths and Legends is a Cobblemon add-on. Its key items act as spawn conditions: carrying the correct item makes the associated encounter eligible when the other conditions are satisfied. Version 1.4 and later rely on a companion datapack for the pre-made encounters. The mod and datapack are separate downloads and both must be present for those encounters.

This is a community-facing reference, not an official project wiki. Always compare the requirements here with the datapack installed on your server.
